Snooze Math

The alarm you set is not the alarm that wakes you.

Boundary: clock arithmetic only Snooze Math adds minutes to clocks and divides margins by snoozes. It never tells you when to sleep, how much sleep you need, or what your mornings say about you.

The last-bell truth

The 6:30 alarm with 3 snoozes of 9 minutes rings last at 6:57. You did not set a 6:30 alarm; you set a 6:57 alarm with a warm-up act.

The slice truth

Three snoozes of 9 minutes is 27.0 minutes of doze in 3 slices. Nobody dreams in 9 minutes.

The margin truth

From the 6:30 alarm to the 7:45 door there are 75 minutes - room for 8 snoozes of 9 with 3 minutes to spare. Snooze number 9 eats the door.

Run your numbers
Snooze Math is exact clock arithmetic over your alarm and your door - minutes and bells, nothing more.
